Information Architecture

Design ready

Website

  1. Home dashboard with search, filters, recommended articles, trending resources, and newsletter prompt.
  2. Content library with category, stage, topic, and keyword filtering.
  3. Article detail pages with save, rate, newsletter, related resources, and reading-time tracking.
  4. Profile and preferences for business stage, sector, preferred categories, notification frequency, and saved items.
  5. Admin CMS, sponsored content, and analytics areas for platform staff.

Mobile App

  1. Home feed with personalised cards, quick filters, bookmarks, and recent searches.
  2. Search tab for keyword entry, saved filters, categories, and recommendation refresh.
  3. Article detail screen with image, summary, reading progress, save, share, and rating actions.
  4. Saved tab for bookmarked articles, guides, tools, and offline-ready resources.
  5. Profile/settings tab for login, preferences, newsletter, privacy, and notification controls.

Shared Services

  1. React website and mobile app clients call the same secured HTTPS REST API.
  2. Node.js/Express handles authentication, content, recommendations, email, analytics, and CMS requests.
  3. MySQL stores users, sessions, articles, categories, preferences, subscriptions, ads, and recommendations.
  4. MongoDB stores searches, clicks, reading time, scroll depth, ratings, and other behaviour events.
  5. Recommendation scoring combines profile data, search history, saved content, ratings, and trending metadata.
